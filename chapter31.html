"Acha, tum use jahannum hi keh kar bulaoge. Hum nightclub gaye the-kya tum saathjate?" Salar ne jawabdiya. "Kyoon nahi?" Saad ka jawab sunkar Salar hairan ho gaya. "Tum jate?""Bilkul!" Saad ne tasdeek ki. "Aur tum wahan kya karte? Na tum peete ho, na tum dance karte ho-tum wahanhumein lecture dete apni baatein?" Salar ne tewar se poocha. "Nahi. Toh main nahi peeta, na dance karta, lekin ek achi outing hoti. Maza aata," Saad ne jawab diya. "Islam aise kaamon se mana karta hai-aisijagaon pe jane se?" Salar ne teekhi awazmein poocha. Kuch dair ki khamoshi ke baad, Saad ne jawabdiya, "Main kuch galat nahi kar raha hota, bas manzar badal raha hota."

Mujhe kuch assignments par kaam karna hai, aur mere sabflat-mates isweekend ghar par honge. Main wahan kaam nahi kar sakta," Saad ne wazahat di. Bottle aur glasses se pata chal raha tha ke wo peeye they: khanepeene ka silsila us ghar mein jise Saad na paak samajhta tha. Salar ko khayal aya ke ek insaan jo khud ko pious aur practicing Muslim sabit karnachahta hai, wo itna bara fraud nikla.
